# Solar System Explorer - Controls

## Movement

- **W** - Move spacecraft forward
- **S** - Move spacecraft backward
- **A** - Strafe left (move left while maintaining view direction)
- **D** - Strafe right (move right while maintaining view direction)

## Camera

- **Mouse Movement** - Rotate camera view
  - Move mouse left/right to look horizontally
  - Move mouse up/down to look vertically

## System

- **ESC** - Exit the application

## Tips

- Fly close to planets to see information about them
- A semi-transparent panel will appear when you're near a planet
- The panel fades in smoothly as you approach and fades out when you leave
- Move slowly near planets to read the information
- The Sun is at the center - all planets orbit around it
- Outer planets move more slowly than inner planets
