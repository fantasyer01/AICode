# Solar System Explorer - Quick Start Guide

## How to Run

1. Open a terminal in the project directory
2. Navigate to the build folder:
   ```
   cd build
   ```
3. Run the executable:
   ```
   SolarSystemExplorer.exe
   ```

## What to Expect

When you launch Solar System Explorer, you'll see:
- A 3D solar system with the Sun at the center
- Eight planets orbiting at different speeds and distances
- A beautiful starfield background
- Your spacecraft floating in space

## Getting Started

1. **Move Around**: Use W/A/S/D keys to fly your spacecraft
   - W: Forward
   - S: Backward
   - A: Left
   - D: Right

2. **Look Around**: Move your mouse to rotate the camera view

3. **Explore Planets**: Fly close to any planet to trigger an information panel
   - The panel appears automatically when you're near a planet
   - It shows educational facts about that celestial body
   - Move away and the panel will fade out

## Tips for Exploration

- Start by flying toward the bright Sun at the center
- Outer planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune) are larger and slower
- Inner planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) are smaller and faster
- Take your time near each planet to read the information
- Use mouse to look around and appreciate the orbital motion

## Troubleshooting

**Can't see anything**: Make sure you're not too close to the Sun. Back away using the S key.

**Movement feels too fast/slow**: The base speed is calibrated for exploration. You can modify it in the code if needed.

**Application won't start**: Ensure your graphics drivers support OpenGL 3.3 or higher.

## Exit

Press ESC at any time to exit the application.

## Educational Goals

By exploring the solar system, you'll learn:
- The names and order of planets from the Sun
- Relative sizes of planets
- How planets orbit at different speeds
- Key facts about each celestial body
- The structure of our solar system

Enjoy your journey through space!
